TACTICAL BRIEFING // RUN FROM DANDO

01 // MOVEMENT & EVASION

• Move: WASD / Arrow Keys or Left Touch Stick.
• Dodge [SPACE]: Directional dash with i-frames. Dando leads his lunges ahead of your movement—dodge across his cone for a Lunge Juke (+140) or dodge approaching vehicles for a Hazard Juke (+75).
• Sprint [L-SHIFT]: Speed burst (requires ≥15% Stamina; on mobile, tap SPRINT to toggle or push stick fully).
• Skill [E]: Triggers your Archetype's tactical ability.

02 // DANDO AI & ANTI-CAMP SONAR

• Dando navigates via 60×45 grid A* and escalates across 5 Heat Waves (every 16s).
• Break line-of-sight behind blocks or inside Smoke Vents to force Dando into quadrant search (note: Dando senses and grabs you if you stand right next to him inside smoke!).
• After the opening 6s grace window, camping motionless for 4.5s triggers Dando's Sonar Ping!

03 // POWER-UPS & FIELD AGENTS

• ⚡ Caffeine Shot: +35% speed (5s).
• 🛡 Riot Shield: Absorbs 1 hit (8s).
• ❄ EMP Slowdown: Slows Dando & traffic by 60% (3.5s).
• 🎭 Holo Decoy: Lures Dando away.
• ✚ Medkit: Restores +1 HP (+100 Overheal Bonus at full HP).
• Lure Dando near ◎ Press for camera distractions; grab drops from ★ Insiders; evade ✖ Checkers.

04 // OPERATION MODES & SEEDS

• Deterministic Seeds: Lock a seed with 🔒 LOCKED to replay the exact same layout, NPC spawns, and AI decisions.
• Time Attack: Collect Score Stars (+250) and juke lunges to hit the target score before time expires.
• Escape Mission: Stand inside 3 open-plaza Terminals to hack them (100%), then reach the North Helipad Exit.
